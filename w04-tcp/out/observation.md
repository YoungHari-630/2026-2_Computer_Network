- For 2,000 bytes, the seed-246 run sent 381 data-channel packets versus the 250-packet minimum, so reliability cost 1.52×; loss caused retransmissions, while delayed duplication was the subtle correctness case.<br>**한국어:** 최소 250개 대신 381개를 보내 신뢰성 비용은 1.52배였고, 손실은 재전송을 만들며 지연된 중복 패킷 처리가 가장 까다로웠다.

## Task 2
- In my phone-hotspot capture, packets 1/2/3 are SYN/SYN-ACK/ACK; the client/server raw ISNs are 3696188880 and 3143740721. The client/server advertised MSS 1460/1400, window scales ×256/×8192, and SACK permitted; randomized ISNs distinguish new connections from delayed old segments and are harder to guess.<br>**한국어:** 내 핫스팟 캡처의 1·2·3번이 3-way handshake이며, 양쪽은 MSS·윈도 스케일·SACK을 협상하고 임의 ISN으로 이전 연결과 새 연결을 구별한다.
- The client's first post-handshake receive window was 255 × 256 = 65,280 bytes and later grew to 261,888 bytes, while server bytes in flight peaked at 62,780. Since the sender stayed below the advertised window, receiver flow control was not the limit; congestion control, RTT, and path capacity were.<br>**한국어:** 최대 전송 중 데이터 62,780바이트가 광고된 수신 윈도보다 작았으므로 수신 버퍼가 병목은 아니었다.
- Dormitory Wi-Fi measured 71.27 Mbps median (61.41–73.67, 17% spread) with a 29.9 ms median handshake; the phone hotspot measured 26.13 Mbps (23.13–28.42, 20% spread) with 32.6 ms. Radio contention, cellular scheduling, and backhaul explain most of the hotspot's lower throughput and spread; its longer RTT also slows the ACK clock and congestion-window growth for a given window.<br>**한국어:** 기숙사 Wi-Fi가 71.27 Mbps, 핫스팟이 26.13 Mbps였으며, 핫스팟의 무선 경쟁과 백홀 용량이 주된 차이이고 더 긴 RTT도 ACK와 윈도 증가를 늦춘다.
